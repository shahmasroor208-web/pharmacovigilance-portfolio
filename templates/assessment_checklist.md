# Case Assessment Checklist

- [ ] Case intake form complete (all required fields)
- [ ] Verify patient identifiers (if applicable) and remove PHI where needed
- [ ] Confirm product identity and exposure details
- [ ] Confirm event description and seriousness
- [ ] Check onset plausibility relative to exposure
- [ ] Assess alternative causes (concomitant meds, underlying disease)
- [ ] Check for duplicates in database / literature
- [ ] Determine expectedness vs product label/SmPC
- [ ] Causality assessment (Not related / Possible / Probable / Definite / Unassessable)
- [ ] Determine reporting requirement (local regulatory / global):
    - [ ] ICSRs / expedited reporting required?
    - [ ] Aggregate reporting required?
- [ ] Identify follow-up information required and owner
- [ ] Assign medical reviewer and QA reviewer
- [ ] Document decision and close or escalate
